\section{The corpus}
\label{sec:corpus}

Release 1.1 of the corpus (Table~\ref{tab:corpus}, Figure~\ref{fig:corpus}) was packaged as one machine-readable JSON record per conversation. Each record contained NARA's metadata and subject log, the audio sources, the withdrawn spans, the speaker segments, and every word with its timing, speaker, confidence and source. The source recorded whether a word came from Parakeet, both recognizers, a Whisper fill, a model edit or a correction by ear. Each edit was stored with the words it replaced and the reason for the change. Plain-text transcripts and the raw output of each recognizer were also included. A browsing application, included in the repository, was built to present the corpus by day, conversation and person and to play each transcript in sync with the audio; no language model wrote its text.
